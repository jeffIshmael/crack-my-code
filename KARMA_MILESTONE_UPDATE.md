## Description (copy for Karma)

We completed our first on-chain Cipher reward campaign on Celo, using a **10 USDT** pool to pay **0.1 USDT** for each win against Cipher AI (our computer opponent).

**Results**
- **+56 players** (75 → 131), **~75% growth**
- **+173 games played** (221 → 394), **~78% growth**
- **~100 wins rewarded** on-chain before the pool was fully distributed
- Payouts sent directly to players’ wallets with on-chain verification

**What we shipped**
- Cipher win rewards (USDT on Celo)
- Daily play limits (5 Cipher games per wallet per day)
- Reward pool status and match history showing past earnings

**Campaign status:** The reward pool is now **depleted**. Cipher remains playable with the daily limit; we are evaluating timing and funding for the next reward phase as we work toward the **500+ player** milestone.

**Next:** Community growth, retention, and preparing round 2 of incentives once we hit the next traction targets.
